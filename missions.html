IROPS Day: the storm scenario

⛈ Winter storm warning · ORD hub · ground stop expected 14:00

A storm is about to hit the Chicago hub. Leadership wants the IROPS Self-Service Rebooking pilot (FLY-4) pulled forward and Gate Change Alerts (FLY-2) hardened for a surge. You have 60 minutes to triage, re-plan, put up quality gates and brief leadership, without breaking your team's metrics.

STATION 1 · 10 MIN · 15 PTS

Situation report

Build one saved filter, SNN · IROPS sitrep, that shows every unresolved item under FLY-2 and FLY-4, plus every open Risk in FLY, sorted so the scariest items are on top.

project = FLY
AND (parent in (FLY-2, FLY-4) OR type = Risk)
AND statusCategory != Done
ORDER BY priority DESC, duedate ASC
  • Bonus (+3): add a second filter showing what in that set is blocked or flagged.
  • Bonus (+2): use Rovo Ask AI to generate the same filter from plain English, then note one difference between its JQL and yours.
  • Bonus (+2): spot the item in your sitrep that regressed or was escalated this week. Hint: FLY-26's priority history.
STATION 2 · 10 MIN · 15 PTS

Turn a risk into work

Risk FLY-47 (push provider rate limits during mass IROPS events) is now likely. In the Hangar, create a Spike SNN · Spike: surge test push provider at 200k alerts / 10 min with labels seat-NN, irops-NN and spike, acceptance criteria and a 2-day time box. In the description, record the relationships you'd link in real life: relates to FLY-47, blocks FLY-21. Flag your task if it needs a decision today.

Debrief: what good looks like

A spike has a question, a time box and a decision output, not a deliverable feature. Good acceptance criteria might be: “Measured throughput and error rate at 50k / 100k / 200k alerts”, “Recommendation: batch vs. priority queue”, “Decision recorded on the risk”. Why text instead of links? A link would show on FLY-21 for everyone and change verified answers such as issueLinkType = "is blocked by". In your real space, you'd link it, then check from FLY-21's side that it says is blocked by your spike.

STATION 3 · 15 MIN · 20 PTS

Re-plan under pressure

FLY-36 (13 points) is too big. Read its comments (the Scrum Master hints at the split), then create the three slices in the Hangar: SNN · IROPS · read-only options, SNN · IROPS · seat hold and SNN · IROPS · one-tap confirm, labeled seat-NN + irops-NN. Then build a plan for JET-2026.q4.4 assuming the crew loses 30% capacity to storm support: from 24 points down to about 17.

  1. Decide what moves out of JET-2026.q4.4 to make room for the IROPS slice and your spike.
  2. Premium: in Plans, create a scenario called SNN storm, reduce team capacity, and screenshot the impact on Mobile 5.3 and Bag Tracker Beta. Never press “Save to Jira”: it would rewrite FLY's sprints for every crew.
  3. Standard: write the same reasoning (sprint, points in, points out, release risk) as a comment on your crew's spike.
Debrief: the trade-offs

Strong answers keep the spike (it de-risks the whole surge), pull in only the read-only IROPS slice (smallest valuable increment), defer the accessibility work with a note on the regulatory date (careful: FLY-26 was just escalated to Highest after an audit, so deferring it needs an explicit decision), and respect FLY-33 → FLY-31. The best crews check the result against the Backlog Readiness rule: is there still 2–3 sprints of Ready work after the re-plan?

STATION 4 · 10 MIN · 15 PTS

Quality gate for the surge

Write the release-readiness query for Mobile 5.3: anything in the release that is not yet Ready for Release or Done, plus any open Bug of High priority or above in FLY-2. Bonus: also list anything that left the release. With Zephyr installed, also create a test cycle SNN · IROPS surge with at least three test cases linked to your crew's IROPS slices in the Hangar. Mention FLY-21 and FLY-25 in the test cases as the behavior under test, but don't link to them.

project = FLY AND fixVersion = "Mobile 5.3"
AND status not in ("Ready for Release", Done)
ORDER BY status ASC
Debrief: expected results

Not release-ready: FLY-21, FLY-24, FLY-25, FLY-26, FLY-34, FLY-35, FLY-50. High/Highest open bugs under FLY-2: FLY-25. Left the release: FLY-23 (moved to Gate Ops 1.0), found with fixVersion WAS "Mobile 5.3" AND (fixVersion != "Mobile 5.3" OR fixVersion is EMPTY).

STATION 5 · 5 MIN · 10 PTS

Automate the next storm

Design on paper a rule for FLY: When a Risk is changed to priority Highest, create a linked Spike under the same parent, flag the Risk, and notify the space lead. Write the trigger, conditions, actions and smart values. If you have rights and want to build it, build it in HGR only, with the condition labels = irops-NN, and test it on one of your own tasks.

Debrief: a reference design

Trigger: Field value changed (Priority). Conditions: issue type = Risk; priority = Highest; project = FLY. Actions: Create work item (Spike) with summary Spike: mitigate {{issue.summary}} and parent {{issue.parent.key}}; Link work items (relates to trigger); Edit Flagged = Impediment; Send notification with {{issue.url}}. Guardrail: add a condition that no Spike already relates to the Risk, so re-triggering doesn't create duplicates. In a shared practice space, the crew-label condition is the guardrail that keeps 20 rules from firing on each other's work.
