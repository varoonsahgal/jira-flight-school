IROPS Day: the storm scenario

⛈ Winter storm warning · ORD hub · ground stop expected 14:00

A storm is about to hit the Chicago hub. Leadership wants the IROPS Self-Service Rebooking pilot (FLY-4) pulled forward and Gate Change Alerts (FLY-2) hardened for a surge. You have 60 minutes to triage, re-plan, put up quality gates and brief leadership, without breaking your team's metrics.

60:00
Rules of the airWork in crews of 2 or 3. Pick a crew code (for example T1) and prefix everything you create with [T1]. Add the label irops-T1 so the instructor can review your work with one query. Don't edit items you don't own: comment and link instead. Plans steps need the Premium site; otherwise do the math by hand and show your working.
STATION 1 · 10 MIN · 15 PTS

Situation report

Build one saved filter, [T1] IROPS sitrep, that shows every unresolved item under FLY-2 and FLY-4, plus every open Risk in FLY, sorted so the scariest items are on top.

project = FLY
AND (parent in (FLY-2, FLY-4) OR type = Risk)
AND statusCategory != Done
ORDER BY priority DESC, duedate ASC
  • Bonus (+3): add a second filter showing what in that set is blocked or flagged.
  • Bonus (+2): use Rovo Ask AI to generate the same filter from plain English, then note one difference between its JQL and yours.
STATION 2 · 10 MIN · 15 PTS

Turn a risk into work

Risk FLY-47 (push provider rate limits during mass IROPS events) is now likely. Create a Spike [T1] Spike: surge test push provider at 200k alerts / 10 min under FLY-2 with acceptance criteria and a 2-day time box. Link it so that it relates to FLY-47 and blocks FLY-21. Flag it if it needs a decision today.

Debrief: what good looks like

A spike has a question, a time box and a decision output, not a deliverable feature. Good acceptance criteria might be: “Measured throughput and error rate at 50k / 100k / 200k alerts”, “Recommendation: batch vs. priority queue”, “Decision recorded on FLY-47”. Check your links from FLY-21's side: it should now say is blocked by your spike.

STATION 3 · 15 MIN · 20 PTS

Re-plan under pressure

FLY-36 (13 points) is too big. Split it into three stories: read-only options, seat hold, and one-tap confirm. The Scrum Master's comment on FLY-36 hints at this. Then build a plan for JET-2026.q4.4 assuming the crew loses 30% capacity to storm support: from 24 points down to about 17.

  1. Decide what moves out of JET-2026.q4.4 to make room for the IROPS slice and your spike.
  2. Premium: in Plans, create a scenario called T1 storm, reduce team capacity, and screenshot the impact on Mobile 5.3 and Bag Tracker Beta.
  3. Standard: write the same reasoning as a comment on FLY-4 (sprint, points in, points out, release risk).
Debrief: the trade-offs

Strong answers keep the spike (it de-risks the whole surge), pull in only the read-only IROPS slice (smallest valuable increment), defer the accessibility work with a note on the regulatory date, and respect FLY-33 → FLY-31. The best crews check the result against the Backlog Readiness rule: is there still 2–3 sprints of Ready work after the re-plan?

STATION 4 · 10 MIN · 15 PTS

Quality gate for the surge

Write the release-readiness query for Mobile 5.3: anything in the release that is not yet Ready for Release or Done, plus any open Bug of High priority or above in FLY-2. With Zephyr installed, also create a test cycle [T1] IROPS surge with at least three test cases linked to FLY-21 and FLY-25.

project = FLY AND fixVersion = "Mobile 5.3"
AND status not in ("Ready for Release", Done)
ORDER BY status ASC
STATION 5 · 5 MIN · 10 PTS

Automate the next storm

Design (on paper or in Automation if you have rights) a rule: When a Risk in FLY is changed to priority Highest, create a linked Spike under the same parent, flag the Risk, and notify the space lead. Write the trigger, conditions, actions and smart values.

Debrief: a reference design

Trigger: Field value changed (Priority). Conditions: issue type = Risk; priority = Highest; project = FLY. Actions: Create work item (Spike) with summary Spike: mitigate {{issue.summary}} and parent {{issue.parent.key}}; Link work items (relates to trigger); Edit Flagged = Impediment; Send notification with {{issue.url}}. Guardrail: add a condition that no Spike already relates to the Risk, so re-triggering doesn't create duplicates.
